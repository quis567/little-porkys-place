Our Story

The Story Behind
the Smoke

It started with a backyard smoker, a family recipe passed down three generations, and the kind of stubbornness that says "I can do this better." A few hundred pounds of pork shoulder later, Little Porky's Place hit the road.

We don't rush anything. Our brisket goes 14 hours low-and-slow. Our ribs get a dry rub nap overnight. Our sauce simmers until it's thick enough to stand a spoon in. It's the way our grandfather made it, and the way we'll keep making it.

Now we bring that same obsession to BBQs, weddings, corporate events, and every Long Island parking lot brave enough to smell like hickory for a day.
