Good to Know

Booking & Service

Lead Time

We recommend booking 7–14 days ahead for hot buffet packages and large events. Same-week appetizer trays and platters are often available — call to check.

Staffing

Grillmaster $170 + $40 per additional hour. Buffet server $150 + $40 per additional hour. 3-hour minimum (1 hour setup, 2 hours service).

Bar & Beverages

Soda & water, coffee & tea available per person. Full bar service is market priced. Alcoholic beverages require 21 days advance notice for a one-day liquor license.
